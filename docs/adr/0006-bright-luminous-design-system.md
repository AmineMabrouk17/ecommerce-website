# ADR-0006: Bright & luminous design system — coral on warm cream, soft-rounded, light + dark

The storefront started as a monochrome zinc palette (near-black primary, white cards, `rounded-md`) that read as cold and unattractive. We adopted a **bright & luminous** direction: a sunset-coral primary (`#EE5A2D`, `#FF7A55` on dark) over warm-cream neutrals (`#FBF7F0`), with a soft-rounded component language — ~1rem base radius, `rounded-2xl` cards, `rounded-full` buttons/pills, soft diffuse shadows, hairline borders — and full light **and** dark token sets exposed through `next-themes` with a header toggle. The name "Lumina" (light) anchors the direction; the rejected alternatives were bold & vibrant, warm & earthy, and premium & sleek. Downstream consequences: hardcoded semantic colors (amber/green/red status classes, star ratings) get pulled into the token system, and the admin area follows the same palette for consistency.
